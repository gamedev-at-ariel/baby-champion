# Baby Champion - Game Design - DRAFT

## Basic definition

Primary player experience: parenting a newly-born baby. A 3-D game.

Target platforms: Web (itch.io) and Android.


## Formal elements

### Players 
Single player. 

Target audience: young people who are going to have a baby or the first time, and want to mentally prepare for the event.
* Future extension: two players (simulating two parents) work in cooperation.

### Objectives

The major objective is that the baby grows up to at least 3 years old (kindergarten level). The game has many different levels, each one corresponds to roughly one month of age. In each level, there is a specific objecive related to that age. Example levels:

* a. Newly born: objecive is simply to keep the baby well-fed and clean. 
* b. A bit older: when you try to feed him, he waves his hands, hits the spoon, and the food flies on the floor. Here, the objective is to bring the spoon to his mouth before he hits it.
* c. A bit older: you sit near your computer, try to get some work done. Your baby crawls and snaps your wireless mouse and runs away with it. The objective is to catch him before he throws the mouse to the toilet.
   More levels, with different objectives, will be added later.

### Procedures
* Moving in the house.
* Taking / putting down the baby.
* Feeding the baby.
* Changing diapers.
* Singing.
* Feeding yourself - so that you have energy for the baby.
* Buying supplies in the store (e.g. diapers, baby food, food for yourself).
* Various other possible procedures depending on the level. 
All procedures can be operated by mouse-click / touch.

### Rules
* The baby becomes hungry with time; hunger above a specific threshold makes him cry. Eating resets the hunger.
* The baby dirties the diaper; A baby with a dirty diaper feels irritated and starts crying, until the diaper is changed.
* A tired baby might start crying, until he is put to sleep.
* Sometimes the baby is to tense and cannot fall asleep; singing might help.
* You yourself become hungry and tired if you do not eat or sleep well.
* When the baby cries, you become anxious and cannot sleep.
* Add more rules, based on realistic facts about babies.

### Resources
* Time. Everything must be done in time, as the baby becomes hungry / tired / bored when time passes.
* Money. You need money to buy baby food, diapers and other stuff.
* Diapers, baby food, adult food. 
* Energy. You need energy to function. You get energy by eating & resting; you lose energy by working. You lose energy faster when the baby is crying.

### Conflicts
* Conflicts with the environment: as time passes, you must care for the baby's needs before he starts crying.
* Conflicts with the baby: the baby might throw things on the floor, dirty the house, break things, snap your stuff out of boredom, etc.

### Boundaries
* The game happens mostly within the house. 
* You can also go out to the store to buy supplies. Not sure whether we should model it, or just assume that we buy supplies by phone. 
* Same for work: should we model that you go to work, or just assume you work from home?

### Outcomes
* In each level, you must accomplish the level objective (e.g. feeding the baby, keeping the baby happy) before you move to the next level. Success means you go to the next level; failure means that you play the level again.


## Development tasks

More levels should be added, reflecting both the difficult and the funny aspects of parenting.
